Booking a venue shouldn't take a week, but quotes arrive as scattered emails, each with different terms. DealRelay. Agents and humans. One deal room. The agency sets the specs once. 500 guests, a $30,000 budget, a 2 a.m. curfew. Two venue proposals arrive, and the agents take it from there. Scout reads each proposal. Finance checks it against budget and curfew, and flags every violation. Negotiator drafts the counter, $27,000, if the curfew moves to $130. Every step is traced, so nothing is a black box. Nothing closes without a human. The client reviews, approves, and the vendor accepts. No follow-up emails, no lost threads. Four days of email, collapsed into 46 seconds. Three agents, one approval, zero lost threads. Durable actors hold the state. Agent Relay coordinates. The UI just mirrors the room. Open source. Find it on GitHub.
